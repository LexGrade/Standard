# LexGrade Trademark Policy

**Last updated: 2026-06-20**

LexGrade is two things: an **open standard** — free to implement, fork, and redistribute under its licenses — and a **commercial product**, the canonical hosted service. The standard is meant to spread; the "LexGrade" name identifies the official implementation. This policy keeps the two from swallowing each other.

## What you can call your work

| Thing | Status | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **"LexGrade"** — word mark, logo, domain | Service mark of the SaaS LLC | Don't use it to brand your own product, service, or domain without written permission. |
| **"LexGrade Standard v1.0"** | Name of the open standard | Use this exact phrase to describe an implementation of the open rubric + Technique Register, as long as you don't imply endorsement. |
| The rubric and Technique Register | Open content (CC-BY-SA-4.0; tooling AGPL-3.0) | Implement, fork, and redistribute freely — under any name except "LexGrade." |

## Without asking, you may

- Implement the open rubric and Technique Register — describe it as "an implementation of the LexGrade Standard v1.0."
- Fork the open tooling under AGPL-3.0 and ship it under your own name (see [FORK.md](FORK.md)).
- Cite "LexGrade" in writing, research, journalism, and reviews — nominative fair use.
- Link to lexgrade.org.

## With written permission

- Branding a product or service "LexGrade …" (e.g. "LexGrade Pro").
- Using the logo or visual identity.
- Using a domain containing "lexgrade" in a way users could confuse with the official service.
- Implying endorsement ("Recommended by LexGrade").

Email **hello@lexgrade.org**, subject `Trademark request: <use case>`. We respond within 30 days; permission is generally granted for nonprofit, academic, and clinic use, and denied where it would create user confusion.

## If you fork

Rename it (anything without "LexGrade"), acknowledge the source — *"Built on the LexGrade Standard v1.0 (lexgrade.org), under AGPL-3.0 / CC-BY-SA-4.0"* — and don't claim endorsement. Full guide in [FORK.md](FORK.md).

## What we will and won't do

- We **won't** police nominative use, bully forks that follow this policy, or sell the mark to a hostile acquirer — the perpetual fork rights in [FORK.md](FORK.md) survive any acquisition.
- We **will** enforce against bad-faith confusion (a paid "LexGrade" knockoff), publish every enforcement action in this repository, and transfer the mark to a neutral fiscal host once the project reaches the scale described in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Status

"LexGrade" is a common-law service mark; a USPTO application is pending. Trademark questions or disputes: **hello@lexgrade.org**.
